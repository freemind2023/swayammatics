Business process automation (BPA) means using software to carry out a repeatable business task — approvals, data entry, reporting — without someone doing it by hand every time.

## What it looks like in practice

- A new vendor invoice automatically routes to the right approver based on amount and department.
- Customer data entered in one system automatically syncs to your CRM and accounting tool.
- A weekly sales report generates and sends itself every Monday morning.

None of these require AI — they're rule-based workflows. That's the point: most automation wins come from structure, not intelligence.

## Signs you're ready for it

- The same multi-step task happens every day or every week.
- Data gets copied by hand between two or more systems.
- A process regularly stalls because it's waiting on one person to notice and act.

## Where to start

Start with the single most repetitive, most time-consuming task your team does. Automating one well-understood process is more valuable than a large automation project that tries to cover everything at once.
