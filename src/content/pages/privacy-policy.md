_Last updated: November 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Swayammatics ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or submit an enquiry through it.

#### Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your full name, business email, phone number, company name, website (optional), industry, company size, the areas you'd like to improve, your project description, and preferred contact method.

If analytics is enabled on this site, we may also collect standard usage data (pages visited, general location, device and browser type) through tools such as Google Analytics.

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and this site is intended for business use.

#### How We Use Information

- To respond to your enquiry and discuss your business requirements
- To maintain an internal record of leads and business enquiries
- To understand how visitors use this website, where analytics is enabled
- To improve our website and the services we offer

We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not add you to marketing email lists simply because you submitted an enquiry — you are only subscribed to marketing communications you explicitly opt into.

#### How Information Is Stored and Processed

Enquiry submissions are stored in our internal CRM (Airtable) and transactional emails are sent via Resend. These providers process data on our behalf, under their own security and privacy practices, solely to help us operate this website and respond to enquiries.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

#### Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain our business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time, as described below.

#### Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us through this site's [contact page](/contact).

#### Cookies

This site may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality and, where enabled, analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

#### Third-Party Links

This website may link to third-party websites, including our product site [HR Wala](https://www.hrwalah.com/). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

#### Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

#### Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our [contact page](/contact).
