A CRM only helps if leads actually get into it, get followed up on, and move through a defined pipeline. In practice, that's where most CRMs fall apart.

## The three most common gaps

1. **Leads never make it into the CRM.** Form submissions, calls, and social messages stay scattered instead of landing in one system.
2. **Follow-up depends on someone remembering to do it.** Without automated reminders or sequences, leads go cold.
3. **Pipeline stages don't reflect reality.** Deals sit in the wrong stage because updating the CRM is extra work nobody prioritises.

## What automation fixes here

- Automatically capturing leads from your website, forms, and other channels straight into the CRM
- Triggering a follow-up sequence the moment a lead is created, so response time doesn't depend on who's free
- Moving deals between pipeline stages automatically based on actions the lead takes (opened an email, booked a call, signed a contract)

The goal isn't a fancier CRM — it's making sure the one you have actually gets used.
